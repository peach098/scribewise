how it works4 steps

Four steps in a bot chat, and the drafts write themselves

  1. Connect Telegram with a QR code

    No phone number, no login code. ScribeWise signs in as one more of your devices, and you can remove it any time.

  2. Pick your work chats

    It reads only those. Chats with family and friends stay yours alone.

  3. It learns your style

    From your own messages over the last 6 months: tone, length, greetings, language. The import is deleted afterwards, keeping only a few examples.

  4. Reply with one tap

    When someone writes, a ready draft is waiting in your bot chat. Send, edit, regenerate or reject in a second.

privacy

Your chats stay yours

We’re asking for access to something personal: your messages. So you don’t have to take our word for it. Here are the facts.

  • Connects as a second device

    Remove it in Telegram under Settings → Devices. The agent’s session ends immediately.

  • Only the chats you pick

    Messages from every other chat are never read or stored.

  • Encrypted, kept for 30 days

    Messages are stored encrypted and deleted automatically after 30 days.

    Check exactly what the agent does with your data, and run it in Docker on your own server with your own API key.

FAQ

Why ScribeWise if Telegram already has AI?

Telegram’s built-in AI does different jobs. AI Styles rewrites text you’ve already written in a chosen style. Chat Automation connects a bot that answers people on its own.

ScribeWise drafts the reply to an incoming message for you, writes it the way you write, based on six months of your messages, and sends nothing without your approval. It also checks each reply against your promises and your Jira and Trello tasks.

Is it safe? Does the agent get full access to my account?

Technically, yes: ScribeWise signs in to Telegram as another of your devices, and Telegram doesn’t let a device see only some chats. So the limits live in the agent itself. It reads and stores only the chats you selected, encrypts messages and deletes them after 30 days.

You can switch it off any time in Telegram under Settings → Devices. The code is open, and the self-hosted version runs entirely on your server.

Can Telegram ban my account?

There is a risk, and we won’t hide it: Telegram is wary of automation on personal accounts. ScribeWise keeps it low where it can: no mass messaging, only your selected chats, human-paced replies, and nothing sent without your approval.

Will people know an AI drafted the reply?

Replies go from your account as ordinary messages, with no tags or signatures. And they sound like you, because the style comes from your own messages.

Which model writes the drafts, and where does my text go?

Drafts are written by Claude from Anthropic. To compose a reply, the conversation from your selected chats is sent to the model provider. Keep this in mind if your company has strict data rules.

With self-hosting you use your own API key and choose the provider yourself.

What if a draft isn’t right?

Tap “Edit” to fix it, “Regenerate” for a new version, or “Reject” and reply yourself. The agent never writes a word to anyone without your approval.

What language does it reply in?

Whatever you usually write in that chat. If you talk to one client in English and to your team in another language, the drafts follow suit.

How much does it cost?

The self-hosted version is free. The cloud version is in early access: beta testers will hear about terms and pricing first.
